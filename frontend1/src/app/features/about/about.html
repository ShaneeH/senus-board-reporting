What is ReportHub?

Financial reporting software is often built either for accountants or for developers. ReportHub is designed for the people in between: board members, investors and decision makers who need the answers, not hundreds of pages of financial statements.

Simply upload a company's financial report and ReportHub will:

  • Identify the company and reporting period automatically.
  • Extract key financial information using AI.
  • Normalise the data into a structured PostgreSQL database.
  • Prevent duplicate document uploads using SHA256 fingerprinting.
  • Enrich existing reporting periods with newly discovered information.
  • Calculate financial KPIs including margins, ratios and other board level metrics.
  • Compare reporting periods to highlight trends and year over year performance.
  • Present everything through a clean, interactive dashboard.

The goal is simple: reduce the time spent searching through reports and increase the time spent understanding the business.
